# Staffing Operations Automation Sprint — Launch Pack

This pack turns the highest-ranked portfolio opportunity into approval-ready sales and delivery material. It does not claim that the existing customer-specific staffing application is a production-ready product.

## Offer ladder

1. **Workflow Diagnostic — $750:** map one staffing workflow, quantify its current cost, identify the safest automation boundary, and deliver a fixed-scope recommendation.
2. **Implementation Sprint — starting at $2,000:** implement one approved workflow only after the diagnostic, data authorization, and acceptance criteria are signed.
3. **Monitoring and Improvement — starting at $400/month:** monitor the delivered workflow, handle bounded failures, and provide an outcome report.

The diagnostic is the only launch-now offer. Implementation and monitoring remain gated by technical feasibility, customer authorization, delivery capacity, and gross margin.

## Assets

- `readiness-audit.md`: source-backed go/no-go finding for the two leading repositories.
- `landing-page.md`: focused, evidence-safe offer copy.

## Conversion path

`landing page → diagnostic application → fit/risk triage → 20-minute scope call → signed diagnostic terms → provider-hosted/manual invoice → collected payment → diagnostic delivery`

Do not route buyers through DobeuCloud's custom payment endpoints until the blockers in `readiness-audit.md` are fixed and independently tested. Do not send outreach or publish customer-derived proof without explicit approval.
