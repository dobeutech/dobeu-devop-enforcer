## Scope-call outline

1. What triggers the workflow and what proves it is complete?
2. How many items run through it per week, and who touches each one?
3. Where do waiting, copying, missing data, exceptions, or mistakes occur?
4. What systems and sensitive data are involved?
5. What must remain a human decision or approval?
6. What would make the diagnostic worth $750?
7. Who can authorize evidence access and approve the result?

End with one of four decisions: fit for paid diagnostic, needs a smaller manual process definition, outside authorized scope, or no current economic case.
